today i laughed out loud in the street outside ursa, which is the first noise i have made in days, because osric's message is such a transparent piece of work — tuesday is a fencing night, it has always been a fencing night, nobody had to invent anything, you just leave the ordinary thing exactly where it is and let a man walk into it. then i said yes. then karin told me laertes had rung the hall this morning to ask about fencing with his own blade, and asked twice about the button. then the man who mows the plot rang, the first time i have heard his voice, and told me yorick is three rows over from dad, which i did not know. i came back to the flat and wrote down that i had said yes and that i knew, so that i cannot come back to the page later and pretend that i did not. sigrid's three boxes are still by the window, unopened, twenty-four days.
