# lisbon — booking, and what was in the file

**22.02** — itinerary from osric at 17:14 on a sunday. a fortnight, not the four days i was told. hotel to 2026-03-06. rosencrantz and guildenstern on the same booking reference.

claudius framed it as the norvik integration work. laertes is already down there. mum said, on the phone before all this, that it would "do me good to be out of the building", which is the kind of thing somebody says when they have been given the sentence to say.

**24.02** — the shared file has voltemand's instructions in it. i do not think anybody meant me to have the folder, i think somebody shared the parent.

read it twice. the second time with the board minutes from the month he died open beside it, and that is when you can see the shape: the resolution dated the 18th, the announcement on the 17th. he does it the same way every time.

then i did the only thing i have done this month without deliberating first. forwarded both pages, with r&g's engagement letter — the one with no requesting director on it — to cornelius and to the chair of the audit committee, from my own account, at ten past eleven at night, subject line *strategic review — for the file*. whatever review was being written about me, the first question it gets asked now is who commissioned the reviewers.

**25.02** — came home on the 19:40, alone, told nobody. r&g still booked to 2026-03-06.

what i felt was not shock. it was the flat relief of being handed proof of a thing i had stopped needing proof of.
