# roskilde — the house

empty since the spring. sigrid kept the keys until she left and then handed them over like they might bite.

cannot be in it for more than an hour. five attempts now and the longest was fifty minutes.

**31.01** — an hour, then left. the fencing kit is in the upstairs hall closet. the plot at the churchyard is 380 a quarter out of the estate account and somebody mows it and i have never met them.

**21.02** — drove out on saturday, sat in the car in the lane for about twenty minutes, did not go in, drove back. that is the fifth time and the first time i have written it down honestly.

**26.02** — the water below the house. that is where they found her.

i am not going to be able to go back there now and i am going to have to go back there on monday.

**27.02** — the man who mows dad's plot rang. first time i have heard his voice in a year of paying him. he is digging on saturday for monday, at the water end, and wanted to know whether the family minded the old hedge coming out. i said i was not family. he said "youre the son", and then that he has done that churchyard since the year i was born, and that yorick is three rows over from dad — "the comms man, used to do the voices at the harvest supper."
