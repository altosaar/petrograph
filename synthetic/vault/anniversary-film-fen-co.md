screening is set for 2026-03-04 itself. company only, ninth floor.

**11.02** — crew in the building. three of them, small kit, tobias apologising to everyone in the lifts.

**12.02** — tobias at 14:00 about the middle section. he wants forty to sixty seconds of building archive to run under sigrid's voiceover, "something with the tower in it, doesnt need to be pretty, needs to be real."

i said i would find him something.

i said it and then i stood in the corridor on nine for about four seconds and understood what i had just agreed to do. #film

**13.02** — watched sigrid's interview on tobias's monitor. four minutes about him, and at the end she cries, properly, on camera, for a man who signed her pay slips. i have not cried once. what is he to her, that she can do that in four minutes and i cannot do it in eleven months.

tobias is cutting the middle from yorick's old tapes. there is one of yorick rehearsing dad's launch speech in dad's voice, and for about three seconds i thought it was dad. [[yorick-comms-quotes]]
